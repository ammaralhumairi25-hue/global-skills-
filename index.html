Who We Are

A premier bridge between
Iraq & the world.

Committed to excellence, trust, and long-term partnerships — bringing the best of the world to the local market.

Global Skills for Trading & Contracting is a premier trading and contracting company headquartered in Baghdad, Iraq. We bridge Iraq with world-class international markets by fostering strategic international alliances that ensure the steady supply of premium products essential for industrial, agricultural, and consumer needs.

Our portfolio spans Iraqi sulphur exports, exclusive commercial representation of premium Spanish olive oil brands, and a comprehensive range of agricultural and veterinary products. Every product line is backed by verified quality standards, transparent documentation, and professional trade execution.
